# Alcove Home

**Find out if your furniture fits before you move.**

Looking at an apartment or a house? Alcove Home tells you whether the furniture you already own will fit. Add the listing's floor plan (or its room sizes), add your furniture once (paste a store link, no tape measure needed), and every piece gets a clear answer:

- ✅ **Fits:** plenty of room.
- ⚠️ **Tight:** it goes in, but something is cramped. For example, there isn't enough space to pull out a desk chair, or the path to the closet is narrow.
- ❌ **Doesn't fit:** it hits a wall, another piece of furniture, or a door.

Each answer comes with a plain reason, like *"the desk chair needs 3 feet behind it but only has 2½."*

## What you can do today

- **Start from a floor plan picture or PDF.** Paste a screenshot of the listing's floor plan, drop in a picture or a PDF, or pick one from your phone. If the PDF has several pages, like a brochure, you pick the page with the floor plan. The app reads the rooms, their sizes, where the doors and windows are, and fixed things like a kitchen island, counters, a fireplace or a radiator.
- **Check what it read.** You'll see the plan with the app's reading drawn on top. Every size is a box you can retype. Anything the app wasn't sure about is highlighted, and sizes that don't match the drawing are pointed out. You can add, move or remove doors, windows and fixed things before you continue.
- **Or start from the listing's text.** Paste lines like "Bedroom 2: 11' x 12'" and the app makes a room from each one. These rooms don't include door positions, so their answers are marked as approximate.
- **Or just type the room sizes**, in feet and inches or in centimeters.
- **L-shaped rooms work too.** Pick "L-shaped", say which corner is missing and how big it is. The app reads L-shaped rooms from floor plans as well, and you check the missing corner before continuing. Furniture in the missing corner is outside the room, and doors and windows can go on the walls at the inside corner. Switching a room between rectangle and L, or changing its size, keeps doors, windows and fixed things where they were.
- **Mark the things that can't move.** Add a kitchen island, counter, radiator, fireplace, bathtub or built-in shelves to a room, with its size and position. Furniture can't go on top of them, and they count when the app checks the space you need to use a piece and to walk through. If one of them is what makes a path too narrow, the app says so instead of blaming your furniture.
- **Add furniture from a store link.** Paste the link to the product page and the app finds its assembled size (not the box size), including whether it's a corner sofa or an L-shaped desk. You check what it found, with a link back to the store page.
- **If a store won't let the app read its page**, take a screenshot of the product's size details and paste that instead.
- **Get instant sizes for popular pieces.** Once a product's size has been checked, the next person who pastes the same link gets it right away (and still gets to check it). To keep these shared sizes honest, a size is only shared once it matches the store's own page or at least two different people agree on it, so one person can't put in a wrong size.
- **Or add furniture without a link**, from a list of common pieces (beds, desks, sofas, dressers…) or by typing your own sizes.
- **Add extras that change the size**, like a monitor arm, a hutch or a table leaf, and switch them on or off. The store's size stays as it is unless you choose to use your own measurement, and the app labels which one it's using.
- **Move things around** by dragging them on the floor plan and turning them. Pieces line up neatly against walls, fixed things and each other.
- **Get suggestions.** If something doesn't fit, the app can suggest a small move or a turn that would help.
- **See how sure each answer is.** If an answer depends on a wall size that was measured from the drawing rather than printed on the plan, it's marked as approximate.
- **Share a plan.** Send someone a link and they see your rooms, where the furniture goes and whether each piece fits. They can look but not move anything, or save their own copy to try changes. The plan travels inside the link itself, so nothing is uploaded and nobody needs an account. The link shows the plan as it was when you shared it (share again after changes), and it doesn't include the original floor plan picture.
- **Keep several listings.** Your furniture list is saved once and reused for every home you look at. The original floor plan picture is kept too, so you can look at it again.
- **Use it on your phone or computer.** It opens in a web browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. No sign-up. Your plans stay on your own device.

## What's coming next

1. **Try the reading on real listings and real store pages.** Reading floor plans and store pages has been checked with samples, but not yet with real listings and stores. That needs access to the reading service, plus a set of real floor plans and store links.
2. **Check the five-minute goal with real people.** A new person should get from a floor plan screenshot to a fit answer in under five minutes. Everything needed is in place, but it hasn't been timed with real users yet.
3. **Save furniture from a store page in one click**, with a browser add-on.
