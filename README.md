# Alcove Home

**Find out if your furniture fits before you move.**

Looking at an apartment or a house? Alcove Home tells you whether the furniture you already own will fit. Add the listing's floor plan (or its room sizes), add your furniture once, and every piece gets a clear answer:

- ✅ **Fits:** plenty of room.
- ⚠️ **Tight:** it goes in, but something is cramped. For example, there isn't enough space to pull out a desk chair, or the path to the closet is narrow.
- ❌ **Doesn't fit:** it hits a wall, another piece of furniture, or a door.

Each answer comes with a plain reason, like *"the desk chair needs 3 feet behind it but only has 2½."*

## What you can do today

- **Start from a floor plan picture.** Paste a screenshot of the listing's floor plan, drop in a picture, or pick one from your phone. The app reads the rooms, their sizes, and where the doors and windows are.
- **Check what it read.** You'll see the plan with the app's reading drawn on top. Every size is a box you can retype. Anything the app wasn't sure about is highlighted, and sizes that don't match the drawing are pointed out. You can add, move or remove doors and windows before you continue.
- **Or start from the listing's text.** Paste lines like "Bedroom 2: 11' x 12'" and the app makes a room from each one. These rooms don't include door positions, so their answers are marked as approximate.
- **Or just type the room sizes**, in feet and inches or in centimeters.
- **Add your furniture** from a list of common pieces (beds, desks, sofas, dressers…) or type in your own sizes. Corner sofas and L-shaped desks work too.
- **Move things around** by dragging them on the floor plan and turning them. Pieces line up neatly against walls and each other.
- **Get suggestions.** If something doesn't fit, the app can suggest a small move or a turn that would help.
- **See how sure each answer is.** If an answer depends on a wall size that was measured from the drawing rather than printed on the plan, it's marked as approximate.
- **Keep several listings.** Your furniture list is saved once and reused for every home you look at. The original floor plan picture is kept too, so you can look at it again.
- **Use it on your phone or computer.** It opens in a web browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app. No sign-up. Your plans stay on your own device.

1. **Try floor plan reading on real listings.** It has been checked with a sample plan, but not yet with real listing screenshots. That needs a set of real floor plans and access to the reading service.
2. **Add furniture from a store link.** Paste a link to the product page and the app picks up the sizes for you.
3. **Share a plan.** Send someone a link to see your layout.
